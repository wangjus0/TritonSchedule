# Product

## Register

product

## Users

UCSD students planning a quarter schedule. They are comparing classes, checking meeting times, and building a usable weekly grid quickly between advising, enrollment, and course discovery.

## Product Purpose

TritonSchedule makes UCSD course search and schedule planning easier. Success means a student can find relevant courses, understand when they meet, add them to a weekly schedule, and save the plan when account features are available.

## Brand Personality

Calm, clear, efficient. The interface should feel trustworthy and focused, with enough Aqua-inspired polish to feel modern without slowing down the planning workflow.

## Anti-references

Do not make it feel like a generic SaaS landing page, a heavy dashboard, or a decorative school-spirit poster. Avoid ornamental gradients, oversized metric blocks, repeated marketing card grids, and UI chrome that competes with course data.

## Design Principles

Focus on the planning task before brand expression.
Use visual polish to clarify state, not decorate inactive content.
Keep course data dense enough to scan and calm enough to trust.
Make guest use feel complete, with account features presented as a natural save/sync upgrade.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target WCAG AA contrast, visible keyboard focus, semantic controls, and reduced-motion support. Color should reinforce status but not be the only way to understand state.
